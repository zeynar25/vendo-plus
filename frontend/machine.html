Ingredient hoppers

Each hopper stands on a 5 kg load cell read by an HX711 amplifier.

What this mock simulates
  • Everything here is simulated in this browser. No load cell is measured, and the ingredient use is the recipe amount, not measured consumption.
  • Each drink takes the grams in Table 3 of the paper (recipe version R2, cups of about 150 ml) from the hoppers. Amounts marked * are researcher-defined assumptions.
  • Each hopper holds up to 1,000 g in a 200 g empty container. The load control sets the true net load, like the HX711 load control in Wokwi.
  • A reading is the median of five raw samples, each with 2 g of Gaussian noise, converted at 0.42 counts per gram, so values move in steps of about 2.38 g.
  • The firmware's ranges apply: below −10 g or above 1,050 g is rejected and sent as null, −10 g to 0 g is sent as 0 (corrected_zero), and above 1,000 g is flagged over_capacity.
  • Every 5 seconds one batch for the three hoppers goes to POST /api/v1/readings with the device key. A batch that fails is not sent again; the next one carries the newest state.
  • Reloading the page reboots the kit: a new session ID, sequence 0. The hoppers keep their contents in this browser. The starting loads on first use (640 g, 180 g, 420 g) are arbitrary demo values.
